# Stat candidates

Clerk's permanent inbox: every feature, joke or event that could be counted goes here as one line, the day it is built (or noticed in a changelog bullet, a note or a chat). Dev adds the stat in src/stats (docs/STATS.md) and cuts the line to docs/ARCHIVE.md. Clerk also marks the ones worth an achievement with `ACH?` plus a suggested name and tier (rare, funny, a milestone or a first; not routine counters); the author and dev decide. Format: `date | what happens | the number to keep (count, first time, longest...) | possible achievement or console line`.

- 2026-11-24 | boss arrive/leave sounds heard | nothing to count | skip
